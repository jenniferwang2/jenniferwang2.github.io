Forty boxes into structured prep. The single biggest change in my success
rate wasn't learning new exploits — it was refusing to run one until I'd
finished enumerating.

## What changed

Early on I collected exploits. Now I collect *questions*: what is this
service, what version, what does it trust, what does it let me reach. The
exploit is usually obvious once those are answered.

## The buddy-list of habits

- Take the screenshot before you need it, not after.
- Log every command to a file. Re-running from memory wastes exam time.
- If I'm stuck 30 minutes, I've stopped enumerating. Go back and read output.

Next: Active Directory sets. That's where I'm weakest.
