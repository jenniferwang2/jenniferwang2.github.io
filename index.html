Introduction

An overview of me

Most of my experience is on the defensive side of security engineering. I spent eight months as a cybersecurity intern at Seaspan, a shipbuilder operating under CMMC and Controlled Goods requirements, where I worked vulnerability management across a few hundred servers, wrote the data-loss policies protecting export-controlled data, and stood up a secure enclave for a controlled program.

What I like are the parts of security that turn into engineering: giving a finding an owner, making a fix repeatable, replacing a manual review with a pipeline. Before security I did research engineering at Amii and led a team of teaching assistants, which is where I learned that the tooling around a job usually matters as much as the job.
